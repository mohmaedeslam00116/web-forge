# Product and surface diagnosis

Do not map an industry to one visual tone. Products within the same category can have different audiences, jobs, trust requirements, and content. Use the questions below to find the design problem before choosing a visual solution.

| Context | Questions to answer | Common design mistake to watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Healthcare / clinic | Is the visitor booking, researching, checking results, or using a clinical tool? What trust and privacy cues are necessary? | Cold blue-on-white as the only trust strategy; stock medical imagery; weak appointment path |
| Law / finance / consulting | Is the surface explaining expertise, comparing terms, handling sensitive records, or requesting contact? What proof is verifiable? | Empty luxury styling; invented trust badges; ornamental legal/financial clichés |
| Restaurant / hospitality | Is the task to discover, inspect a menu, book, order, or plan a visit? Which details are time-sensitive? | Atmospheric imagery that hides hours, location, menu, or price |
| SaaS / developer product | Is this a marketing page, docs, setup flow, workspace, analytics tool, or admin surface? What is the actual workflow? | Applying one SaaS landing template to the marketing site, dashboard, and docs |
| Portfolio / cultural archive | Is the goal to inspect work, navigate a collection, read context, or contact the creator? | Centered bio hero and identical thumbnail cards regardless of the work |
| Commerce | Is the visitor discovering, comparing, configuring, or checking out? Which attributes change the purchase decision? | Generic product cards, weak product detail, hidden shipping and return information |
| Education | Is the visitor choosing a course, learning in sequence, reviewing progress, or searching material? | Treating a learning tool like a marketing page; progress without actionable feedback |
| Public / nonprofit | Is the task to understand an issue, find a service, verify impact, donate, or access support? | Overly emotional design that obscures facts, eligibility, or next steps |
| Events | Is the surface for discovering, registering, viewing a programme, or navigating a live event? | Countdown-first design when practical programme and location information matter more |
| Agency / studio | Is the goal to qualify a lead, evaluate work, understand capabilities, or contact a team? | Generic agency copy, invented client logos, and parallax used as identity |

## Local and bilingual context

Use country, language, currency, local contact patterns, and cultural conventions only when they are relevant and supported by the brief. For Arabic interfaces, design from Arabic reading order and actual Arabic typography; do not merely mirror an English layout. Do not assume all Arabic-speaking audiences share the same dialect, numeral system, or preferred contact channel.
