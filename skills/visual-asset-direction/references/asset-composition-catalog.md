# Asset composition catalog

Choose composition according to subject and content. These are useful art-direction moves, not a checklist to put on every page.

| Composition | What it does | Good use |
|---|---|---|
| Full-bleed subject | Lets one image, video, or 3D scene own the frame | Campaign, launch, destination, fashion, film |
| Split editorial | Pairs a strong image/artifact with context and proof | Case study, editorial feature, product explanation |
| Product close-up | Uses material/detail to demonstrate quality or capability | Retail, devices, food, industrial products |
| Image mosaic / gallery | Shows breadth, collection, or visual discovery | Portfolio, culture, marketplace, travel |
| Annotated product capture | Connects a real interface or artifact to explanations | SaaS marketing, onboarding, product education |
| Diagram-led explanation | Makes a process or relationship visible | Developer tools, science, education, enterprise |
| Map / spatial scene | Makes location or topology actionable | Travel, fleet, logistics, facilities |
| Collage / cutout | Creates expressive identity through deliberate layering | Campaign, music, youth culture, editorial |
| Poster / typographic art | Turns copy into a designed artifact with supporting graphics | Event, culture, campaign, announcements |
| Texture / material field | Establishes a tactile atmosphere or product world | Luxury, craft, heritage, hospitality |
| Video demonstration | Shows motion or operation that a still cannot explain | Product demos, sport, entertainment |
| Interactive model | Allows inspection or configuration of a meaningful object | Product configurator, architecture, 3D tools |

A composition is only successful when it has a clear subject, meaningful crop, readable hierarchy, responsive strategy, and a valid asset source.
